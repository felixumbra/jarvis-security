The Golden Rules

Memorize these three principles and you will virtually eliminate your risk of payment fraud.

  • 1️⃣ PIN is ONLY for sending money. Never to receive.
  • 2️⃣ Never share your screen or download AnyDesk for "support".
  • 3️⃣ Always verify the receiver's name before typing your PIN.
💸

The Fake Refund

Scammers send a "Collect Request" to your app, disguising it as a refund. If you tap approve and enter your PIN, money leaves your bank.

📷

QR Code Traps

Scanning a QR code is a "push" action strictly used to pay someone else. Never scan a code expecting to receive funds.

🚨

Emergency Protocol & Helplines

If compromised, freeze your account immediately through your bank app. Report cyber fraud directly to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in.
